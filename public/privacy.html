Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This page lists every server your browser contacts when you open mc.trebeljahr.com, and what each one receives. The game runs in your browser. It has no accounts and no forms, and it sets no cookies.

Controller

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the service provider named in the imprint: Rico Trebeljahr, c/o Block Services, Stuttgarter Str. 106, 70736 Fellbach, Germany. Email for privacy questions: imprint+mc@trebeljahr.com.

Servers your browser contacts

  • mc.trebeljahr.com: the game, served by Cloudflare. The page, the game code, the block textures and the style sheets all come from here.
  • static.cloudflareinsights.com: the script of Cloudflare Web Analytics. Visitors in the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland do not get it.
  • plausible.trebeljahr.com: my own Plausible Analytics server. Only the game page loads it, not the imprint or this page.

No other server receives a request from your browser. A link to another site, such as the donation page on ricos.site, contacts that site only when you click it.

Hosting by Cloudflare

The game is a set of static files. Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, serves them from its Worker minecraft-clone. The Worker holds only the files, and no code of mine runs on a server when you load a page. The game code runs in your browser.

Cloudflare receives your IP address, the address of the page, the user agent and the referrer of each request. It uses them to deliver the game and to protect it against abuse. I do not export or keep logs of these requests.

The game uses the fonts installed on your device. Its code, its textures and its style sheets come only from mc.trebeljahr.com.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in delivering the site securely).

Cloudflare Web Analytics

Cloudflare adds the script of Cloudflare Web Analytics to each page. Your browser loads it from static.cloudflareinsights.com, and the script sends its reports to mc.trebeljahr.com/cdn-cgi/rum.

The script sends one report when the page has loaded and one when you leave it. The reports hold the page URL, the referrer, and the version of your browser and of your operating system. They also hold figures for the memory that the page's scripts use, and how long the page took to load and to react.

The timings include Largest Contentful Paint, Interaction to Next Paint and Cumulative Layout Shift. A report names the page element behind a timing, such as #menuTitle. Cloudflare shows me totals by page, country, browser and device type. The reports hold nothing about what you build in the game.

The script sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It gives each page view a random ID and keeps no ID from one page view to the next. Cloudflare receives your IP address with the report, discards it at the nearest data center and does not store it.

Cloudflare does not add the script for visitors who connect through a Cloudflare data center in the EU, the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. For all other visitors the legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in knowing how fast the pages load). You can object at any time, or block static.cloudflareinsights.com with a content blocker.

Plausible Analytics

I count visits with Plausible Analytics. It runs on my own server at plausible.trebeljahr.com, rented from Hetzner Online GmbH in Nuremberg, Germany. Requests to it pass through Cloudflare as described under Hosting.

Your browser loads the Plausible script from plausible.trebeljahr.com and sends its events there. That server receives your IP address and user agent. I do not route these requests through mc.trebeljahr.com, so a content blocker can stop them. The game works the same without them.

Only the game page loads the script, and only on mc.trebeljahr.com. Local and test copies of the game do not load it. The imprint and this page do not load it.

Plausible sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. The script reads one entry in your local storage, plausible_ignore. That entry exists only if you set it yourself to switch counting off.

Plausible combines your IP address and user agent with a salt that changes every day, and stores only the resulting hash. It deletes the salt after 24 hours, so nobody can trace the hash back to you. It never stores the IP address.

The script sends three kinds of events. A page view goes out when the game page opens. A click on a link to another site goes out with the address of that link. When you switch to another tab or window, or leave the page, an event reports how long the page had your attention and how far you scrolled.

Plausible records the page address, the referrer, the browser, the operating system, the device type and your approximate location. The location is the country and, where Plausible's database knows them, the region and city. Plausible derives it from the IP address and then discards the address.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in knowing how many people play the game). You can object at any time, or block plausible.trebeljahr.com with a content blocker.

The donation link

The "Donate" button opens ricos.site in a new tab. Your browser contacts that site only when you click the button. The address of the button contains the address of the game page, so the donation page can send you back. Plausible records the click, with that address. The donation page has its own privacy policy.

Data in your browser

The game writes up to two entries to your browser's local storage. Neither one leaves your device, and you can delete both when you clear this site's data in your browser.

  • world: the chunks of the world that you changed by mining a block. The game writes it each time you mine a block, and reads it when it starts.
  • donation-supported-at: the time you came back from the donation page on ricos.site. The game writes it only when the address has ?supported=1. Nothing reads it yet.

The game uses no cookies, no session storage, no IndexedDB, no Cache Storage and no service worker.

Legal basis: § 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG for the first entry (the game stores the changes you make to the world). The second entry records an action of yours.

Transfers outside the EU

Cloudflare, Inc. is established in the United States. It handles each request in a data center of its network, normally the one nearest to you. Access by U.S. authorities under U.S. law cannot be fully excluded. My Plausible server is in Germany.

Storage period

  • Plausible keeps its events with no time limit. Each event carries only the daily hash, which nobody can link to you once the salt is deleted after 24 hours.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics keeps totals by page, country, browser and device type. Cloudflare does not store the IP address.
  • The entries in your local storage stay there until you delete them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you (Art. 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16);
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17), unless a legal obligation requires me to keep it;
  • restrict processing (Art. 18);
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), which includes both kinds of analytics above.

To use any of these rights, email imprint+mc@trebeljahr.com. You can also complain to a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for me is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg.

The game makes no automated decisions about you and builds no profile of you (Art. 22 GDPR).

Changes

I update this page when the game starts or stops contacting a server, or changes what it keeps in your browser. The date at the top shows the last change.
